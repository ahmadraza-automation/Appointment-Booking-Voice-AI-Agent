# Sample Conversation Flow

**Caller**: Hello  
**Agent**: Assalam o Alaikum! Main aapki appointment booking assistant hoon. Aaj aapko kis cheez ke liye appointment book karna hai?

**Caller**: Mujhe doctor se milna hai  
**Agent**: Theek hai. Pehle aapka pura naam bata dijiye?

**Caller**: Ahmad Raza  
**Agent**: Ahmad Raza, sahi hai? Ab apna phone number bataiye, main digit by digit confirm karunga.

**Caller**: 03001234567  
**Agent**: 0-3-0-0-1-2-3-4-5-6-7. Sahi hai na? Ab bataiye appointment kis wajah se chahiye?

**Caller**: General checkup  
**Agent**: General checkup ke liye. Kal subah 10 baje, kal dopahar 2 baje, ya parso subah 11 baje — kaunsa time theek rahega?

**Caller**: Kal subah 10 baje  
**Agent**: Perfect. Confirm kar raha hoon:  
Naam: Ahmad Raza  
Phone: 03001234567  
Reason: General checkup  
Time: Kal subah 10 baje  
Sab theek hai?

**Caller**: Haan  
**Agent**: Bohot shukriya Ahmad Raza! Aapki appointment confirm ho gayi hai. Team member aapse contact karega. Allah Hafiz!
